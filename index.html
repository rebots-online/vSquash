PHYSICAL CHESS • HOW TO PLAY

Controls, Off-Back-Wall Drives & Pose Cam

1. Do I Have to Move My Player?

Your choice! Toggle AUTO-T-RETURN in the top-left bar (or press T):

  • AUTO-T-RETURN: ON (Default — Focus on Shots & Timing): Your Mii automatically moves to intercept the ball (including stepping into the back pocket to let deep drives come off the back glass wall) and automatically recovers to the T-Position after each shot. You can focus 100% on shot selection and disguise!
  • AUTO-T-RETURN: OFF (Manual Footwork): You control every step using WASD or Arrow Keys (or Webcam Body Lunges). If you stay stranded in a corner without recovering to the T, the AI will attack the open court!

2. How Do I Swing & Shape Shots?

  • Hold & Release (Spacebar or Left-Click):
    • Hold down as the ball approaches to coil your backswing preparation (shows a hard-drive posture that pushes the opponent deep).
    • Release normally → Tight Straight / Cross-Court Drive.
    • Hold Shift (or Right-Click) on release → Opens the racket face (+34° cut) for a Disguised Soft Drop into the front nick!
  • Direct Shot Hotkeys (Instant Swing): 1 Drive · 2 Cut Drop · 3 or B 2-Wall Boast (Shift+B Back-Wall Boast) · 4 High Lob · 5 Volley Kill · Q/E Aim Left/Right Wall.
  • Mouse / Trackpad Swipe: Drag & release on court (Swipe Up = Drive, Short/Shift = Drop, Wide Sideways = Boast, Fast Downward Snap = Volley Kill).

3. Returning the Deep "Off-the-Back-Wall" Drive

In squash, an ideally placed deep drive bounces once in the back court, hits the Back Glass Wall, and rebounds forward and upward.

  • Wait for the Glass Pop: Do not lunge at a deep drive while it is racing backward past you! Let it hit the back glass wall first.
  • Strike on the Upward Rise: As the ball rebounds forward off the glass and kicks upward (watch for the "OFF BACK WALL! • STRIKE RISING" cue), press/release Spacebar, Click, or 1 to unleash a Rising Off-the-Back-Wall Drive ★!

4. Webcam Pose Estimation (Front Cam & Back Cam / TV)

Click 📷 Pose Cam in the bottom-right dock (or Setup menu) to play with live computer vision:

  • Arm & Forearm Swing Tracking: Whip your hand/forearm across the camera to strike! Fast forward/horizontal swing = Drive; gentle downward open chop = Cut Drop; wide sweep = Boast; high snap = Volley Kill.
  • Full-Body T-Position Lunges: Lean or step Left, Right, Forward, or Back in the camera frame to lunge from the T.
  • 🔄 Front Cam vs Back Cam (Big Screen): Click Front Cam / Back Cam next to the Pose button to switch between facing your laptop/phone (Front Cam) or outputting to a TV and tracking your body from behind (Back Cam).
